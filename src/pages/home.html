Questions

Frequently asked

Where is Up Top Studios?
We're on the edge of the countryside near St Albans in Hertfordshire. St Albans City, Harpenden and Hemel Hempstead stations are all close by, and each has fast trains to central London (about 20–30 minutes). We'll send exact directions when you book.
Do you provide a camera operator and engineer?
Yes. Crewed sessions include an engineer who runs the cameras, lighting and sound, so you can focus on the conversation. If you have your own crew, you can dry hire the studio instead.
How quickly will I get my files?
Raw audio and video files are usually ready on the day. Edited episodes and short social clips typically take a few working days, and we can agree a faster turnaround if you need one.
How many people can you record at once?
Our podcast sets comfortably seat up to four people on camera. For larger groups, live sessions or bands, we can open up the large live room.
Can we come and see the studio first?
Of course. Get in touch to arrange a viewing or a quick video walkthrough before you book.

Have a project in mind?

Tell us what you're making and we'll come back with options and a fixed quote within one working day.
