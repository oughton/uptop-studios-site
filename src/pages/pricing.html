FAQ

Booking & payment

How do I book?
Send us an enquiry with your preferred dates. We'll confirm availability and a fixed quote, then hold the date once you've confirmed.
Do you offer discounts for regular bookings?
Yes. Recurring podcast series and multi-day bookings get reduced rates. Tell us how often you plan to record.
Can we invoice through our company?
Yes. We invoice businesses directly and can work with purchase orders.
Can we extend a session on the day?
If the studio is free, you can extend at the hourly rate.
