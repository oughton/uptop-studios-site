The studio

Natural light, countryside quiet, serious kit.

Up Top is a newly developed creative studio on the outskirts of London in Hertfordshire. It's a relaxed place to make things, with the equipment to make them well.

Spaces

What's here

Podcast & film studio

A pre-lit space with several sets: armchairs, a sofa and a round table. It's acoustically treated and ready for multi-camera filming.

Large live room

Fits a full band for live tracking or filmed live sessions. Movable gobos let us shape the acoustics.

Control room

Built around the Yamaha PM1000 console and UAD Apollo interfaces, with ADAM A7X monitoring and synths within reach.

Writing room & lounge

A bright, comfortable room for writing sessions, client breaks and a quiet place to prepare before you go on camera.

Getting here

Easy to reach from London

We're on the edge of the countryside near St Albans. There are three mainline stations nearby, each a short taxi ride from the studio. We'll send exact directions when you book.

St Albans CityThameslink from St Pancras, Farringdon and London Bridge · ~20 min
HarpendenThameslink from St Pancras · ~25 min
Hemel HempsteadLondon Northwestern from Euston · ~25–30 min

By car, we're within easy reach of the M1, M25 and A414.
