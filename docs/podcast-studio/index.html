FAQ

Podcast studio questions

What do I need to bring?
Just your notes and your guests. If you want branded elements on set, such as a logo, product or backdrop, let us know beforehand and we'll build them into the set.
Can you record remote guests?
Yes. We can bring remote guests in over a video call and record them alongside your in-studio hosts.
What file formats do you deliver?
We deliver separate high-resolution video files for each camera and separate WAV audio for each microphone, through a download link. Edited episodes come as MP4 in landscape and vertical formats if you need them.
Do you edit and publish podcasts?
We offer editing, captions, thumbnails and short clips for social media. Publishing to your podcast host and YouTube channel can be arranged.

Planning a podcast series?

Tell us the format and how often you publish, and we'll suggest a recording schedule and package.
